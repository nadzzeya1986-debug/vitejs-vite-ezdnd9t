Pulse Privacy Policy

Effective August 16, 2026

What Pulse stores

Pulse stores account information such as email, username and profile data, plus messages, files and other content that you choose to send through the service. Authentication is handled by Supabase.

Calls and microphone/camera

Voice and video calls require access to your device microphone and, for video calls, camera. Browser and Android permissions control this access. Call signaling is exchanged through the Pulse backend; media is intended to travel peer-to-peer when the network allows.

Notifications

If enabled, Pulse may store a push-subscription endpoint and the keys needed to deliver notifications to your device.

Your choices

You can stop using the service, revoke device permissions and request deletion of your account and associated content through the service operator.
